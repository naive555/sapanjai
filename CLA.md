## What this actually does, in plain language

Sapanjai is offered under two licenses: the AGPL-3.0, to everyone, and a paid commercial
license to organizations that cannot accept the AGPL — see [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).
Offering the second one is only lawful if the project has the right to license *every*
part of the codebase that way, including your contribution.

So this agreement asks you for that right, and nothing beyond it:

- **You keep your copyright.** The license you grant is non-exclusive. Your contribution
  stays yours, and you may use, publish, sell, or relicense it however you like elsewhere.
- **You are not signing your work over.** There is no assignment of ownership here.
- **You are permitting the dual license.** Your contribution may ship both in AGPL
  releases and in commercially licensed ones.
- **You are stating that the work is yours to give** — that you wrote it, or have the
  right to submit it, and that you are not smuggling in someone else's code.

Signing is a one-time comment on your first pull request. It covers your later
contributions too.

## How to sign

You do not need to email anything or send a scan. On your first pull request, a bot will
comment asking you to sign, and you reply on the pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, the commit, and a timestamp are recorded in the `cla-signatures`
branch of this repository. That record is the signature; the reply comment itself is not
stored anywhere else. Subsequent pull requests are recognised automatically.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** If your employer owns your work product and has
not waived its rights, section 4 is not satisfied by an individual signature — open an
issue and a Corporate CLA will be arranged before your pull request is merged.

**Would rather not sign?** Say so on the issue. A clear description of the bug or the fix
is usually enough for the change to be written independently, and you will still be
credited in the commit message and release notes.
